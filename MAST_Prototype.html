The Global AMR Crisis & Diagnostic Limitations

Standard AST relies on hyper-nutritious Mueller-Hinton broth. Inside the host, bacteria encounter nutrient constraints that trigger metabolically conditional resistance states — producing dangerous false susceptibility scores.

1 — The Flaw

Resistance is conventionally treated as a static, genetically encoded trait. In reality, it is a metabolically conditional phenotype regulated by nutrient availability.

2 — The Mechanisms

M1: Glutamine depletion → CpxA/R → OmpF closure → reduced drug influx.
M2: Starvation → ppGpp → H-NS dissociation → PCN amplification (3×–89×) → neutralization.

3 — The Math

Vin > 4(Ve + Vh)

Therapeutic influx threshold at MIC

4 — Environment Split

Lab: High glutamine → OmpF open → artificial sensitivity.
Host: Low glutamine → OmpF closed → clinical resistance.

The Two-Mechanism Model

Gram-negative bacteria orchestrate a synchronized defense inside the host. Mechanism 1 regulates membrane permeability; Mechanism 2 regulates enzymatic degradation and efflux.

Mechanism 1

Nutrient-Driven Porin Gating

Outer membrane permeability is governed by environmental glutamine availability.

Glutamine → Inosine → CpxA-P → OmpR → OmpF ↑
Mechanism 2

Cryptic Gene Derepression & PCN Dynamics

H-NS silences cryptic resistance genes. Starvation triggers derepression and PCN amplification.

Starvation → ppGpp ↑ → H-NS dissociation → PCN amplification

Project Abstract

Metabolism-Aware Susceptibility Testing Framework for Next-Generation Clinical Decision Support in Antimicrobial Resistance

Authors: Muhammad Tasnimul Hasan*, Mahfuza Siddika Khan

Affiliation: Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Manarat International University, Bangladesh

Standard AST fails to replicate the host's metabolic microenvironment, producing systematic false-susceptibility profiles. We propose MAST, a computational framework linking host nutrient availability with CpxA/CpxR-mediated OmpF regulation and ppGpp-mediated stringent responses. The three-module architecture — feature curation, metabolic modeling, and ML classification — is instantiated as this interactive CDSS prototype. As a design-phase artifact, this paper presents the architectural foundation and a rigorous, testable evaluation protocol.
