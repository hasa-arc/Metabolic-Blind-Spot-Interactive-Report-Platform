The Global AMR Crisis & Diagnostic Limitations

Standard Antimicrobial Susceptibility Testing (AST) relies on hyper-nutritious Mueller-Hinton broth. Inside the human body — particularly the gastrointestinal tract — bacteria encounter severe nutrient constraints that trigger metabolically conditional resistance states, producing dangerous false susceptibility scores.

1 The Flaw — WHAT

Antimicrobial resistance is conventionally treated as a static, genetically encoded trait. This is incomplete. Resistance is a metabolically conditional phenotype regulated by environmental nutrient availability.

  • ✕
    Standard AST: Forces metabolic hyper-activation, artificially inflating OmpF porin density and drug influx (Vin).
  • ✓
    Host Reality: Nutrient-constrained niches cause OmpF closure and derepress silent resistance genes (tetA, aadA).

2 The Molecular Mechanisms — HOW

Mechanism 1 — Membrane Permeability

Glutamine depletion → CpxA/R signaling → OmpF ↓ → reduced β-lactam, tetracycline, and aminoglycoside influx.

Mechanism 2 — Cryptic Gene Derepression

Starvation → ppGpp ↑ → H-NS dissociation → PCN amplification (3×–89×) → enzymatic neutralization.

3 The Math & Diagnostic Failure — WHY

Vin > 4(Ve + Vh)

Therapeutic influx threshold at MIC

A marginal porin reduction excludes enough antibiotic to confer resistance — without any genetic mutation.

5 Biomedical Action — WHAT NOW

Bridging the void requires four complementary innovations:

  • ① Host-Mimicking Media (HMDM) — replace Mueller-Hinton with gut-relevant nutrients
  • ② Bio-MEMS Single-Cell Assays — real-time Vin vs. clearance
  • ③ Metabolomic Adjuvants — exogenous glutamine to force OmpF open
  • ④ Computational Flux Modeling — patient-specific predictive algorithms

6 The Bottom Line — SO WHAT

"Metabolic state is not a confounding variable in diagnostic testing — it is the variable."

The Two-Mechanism Model

Gram-negative bacteria orchestrate a synchronized defense strategy inside the human host. Mechanism 1 regulates membrane permeability; Mechanism 2 regulates enzymatic degradation and efflux through epigenetic derepression.

Mechanism 1

Nutrient-Driven Porin Gating

Outer membrane permeability is governed by environmental glutamine availability.

Signaling Cascade
Glutamine → Inosine → CpxA-P → OmpR → OmpF ↑
  • High Glutamine (Lab): OmpF open → Vin exceeds clearance → cell death (artificial susceptibility).
  • Low Glutamine (Host): OmpF closed → Vin drops below threshold → survival.
Mechanism 2

Cryptic Gene Derepression & PCN Dynamics

H-NS proteins silence cryptic resistance genes under lab conditions. Host starvation triggers derepression.

Epigenetic Unlocking
Starvation → ppGpp ↑ → H-NS dissociation → PCN amplification
  • Alarmone Signaling: ppGpp accumulation initiates stringent response.
  • PCN Escalation: Plasmid replication multiplies gene dosage (tetA, aadA), neutralizing internalized drugs.

Project Abstract

Metabolism-Aware Susceptibility Testing Framework for Next-Generation Clinical Decision Support in Antimicrobial Resistance

Authors: Muhammad Tasnimul Hasan*, Mahfuza Siddika Khan

Affiliation: Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Manarat International University, Bangladesh

Corresponding Author: muhammadtasnimulhasan01@gmail.com

Antimicrobial resistance is a critical threat to the UN Sustainable Development Goals, disproportionately impacting low-resource settings such as Bangladesh. Standard antimicrobial susceptibility testing (AST) is performed under highly controlled laboratory conditions that fail to replicate the host's metabolic microenvironment. This mismatch creates a "metabolic blind spot," producing systematic false susceptibility profiles that lead to in vivo treatment failure. We propose Metabolism-Aware Susceptibility Testing (MAST), a computational framework that examines how infection-relevant metabolic conditions influence susceptibility interpretation. The MAST architecture links host-relevant nutrient availability with bacterial resistance mechanisms, including CpxA/CpxR-associated regulation of OmpF permeability and ppGpp-mediated stringent responses. The framework comprises three interconnected modules: (1) metabolic feature curation from public repositories; (2) genome-scale metabolic state modeling; and (3) supervised machine learning classification of conditional resistance phenotypes. We instantiate the framework as an interactive web-based prototype. As a design-phase artifact, this paper presents the architectural foundation and a rigorous, testable evaluation protocol.
